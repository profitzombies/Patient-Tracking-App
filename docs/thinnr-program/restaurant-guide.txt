THINNRRESTAURANT GUIDE
EATING OUT,
THE THINNR WAY
A practical guide to staying on protocol when dining away from home.
BEFORE YOU ORDER
• If a restaurant does not appear on this list, (McDonald’s, Chipotle, Carl’s jr.) It is because they currently do not offer an unbreaded
protein that fits the Thinnr protocol.
• Only eat 5-6 oz of protein- take leftovers home - ASK IN ADVANCE, most restaurants are happy to box part of a larger cut of meat
at time food is served to the table so you are not tempted to eat any extras!
• Skip salad dressing and substitute pico de gallo, or bring your own Walden Farms dressing or other 0 calorie dressing. 1 Oz. Single
serving Walden Farms packets are available on Amazon...so keep a few in your car or purse for these occasions.
• If you plan on being out for a meal, grab an apple from home in case your restaurant options don’t include fruit. With people on
so many specialty diets currently, most restaurants don’t mind you bringing a food item to supplement your needs as long as it
doesn’t disrupt the restaurant.
