- [ ]  Gate/hide marketing materials, training, and marketing files for software-only provider accounts
- [ ]  Create a separate agreement for software-only providers (distinct from the thinner reseller agreement)
- [ ]  Define and implement 3-tier pricing structure for software providers based on patient volume, with varying processing fees per tier
- [ ]  Implement automatic tier upgrade when a provider's patient count exceeds the upper limit of their current tier (e.g., auto-upgrade at patient 101)
- [ ]  Confirm readiness to deliver the minimum viable software account type (gated marketing + training) by end of next week
- [ ]  Share the pricing deck (with tier details) with all participants

### Software-Only Provider Account Type

- The core question was: what features need to change for "software-only" providers vs. thinner resellers
- Consensus reached: the onboarding flow is **the same** for both provider types — the distinction is billing timing and access, not a different flow
- Software-only providers will have the same app experience **except**:
    - No access to marketing materials, training, and files (hidden/gated on the dashboard)
    - Billing starts **immediately** upon sign-up
- Thinner resellers by contrast:
    - Retain access to marketing, sales, and training materials
    - Billing starts **12 months** after their reseller agreement is signed
- The goal for a minimum viable launch: lock those 2 dashboard sections and ship — described as the "iPhone version"

### Pricing Structure

- Matt outlined **3 pricing tiers** at approximately $200, $350, and $500/month
- Tiers are based on **number of patients/users**, not feature gating
- The **only variable between tiers is processing fees**
- Desired billing flexibility includes:
    - Ability to delay billing start by 2–4 months
    - Ability to apply percentage discounts for a defined period (e.g., 50% off for 3 months)
- Ideal automated behavior: when a provider adds a patient that exceeds their tier's limit, **billing automatically upgrades** to the next tier without manual intervention
- Initial tier selection would be handled by the **sales rep**, who then sends the corresponding agreement

### Patient Program Routing

- Patients select their program (e.g., thinner, GLP, carnivore, Mediterranean) upon setup, and the app routes accordingly
- There is **no difference in patient-side logic or onboarding paths** between programs — same flow regardless
- The AI already has diet/program knowledge baked in; uploading individual diet content is not a current priority

### Knowledge Base / Training Portal

- Brief discussion about adding a knowledge base or guide set for how to use the platform
- Deferred — noted as something that could come later
