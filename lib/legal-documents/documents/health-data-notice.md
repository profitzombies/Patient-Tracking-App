# Health data notice

THINNR can connect to Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) so measurements you already record on your phone fill in automatically. The connection is optional, off until you turn it on, and read-only.

## What THINNR reads

- **Weight** — body weight samples, so your weigh-ins appear in the diary and progress views without retyping.
- **Steps** — daily step counts, shown alongside your check-ins.

Those two permissions are the only health permissions the app requests, and they match what the app asks for in your device settings.

## What THINNR never does

- It **never writes** to Apple Health or Health Connect. Nothing you enter in THINNR is pushed back to your phone's health store.
- It does not read any other health category: no heart rate, sleep, workouts, nutrition, cycle, or clinical records.
- It does not use health platform data for advertising, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers.

## How the values are used

Weight and step values are stored in your THINNR program record, visible to you and to your office's care team, under the Privacy Policy. They count as health data you have agreed to let THINNR process.

## Turning it off

Disconnect at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → THINNR, or in Health Connect → App permissions → THINNR. Values already saved in your program record stay until you delete them or close your account.
