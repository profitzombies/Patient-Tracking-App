THINNR App: What is new about the app, and which features or capabilities would you most like us to highlight?
We're taking that from a companion to the THINNR protocol to an operating system for weight loss and wellness within a practice: a fully personalized experience for every single patient, down to their blood work, their wearables, and their genetic information.
Patients will be able to upload all the aforementioned data points, and the AI coach will build a unique plan specifically for them: weekly meal plans, weekly macro target calculations. They'll also be able to select specific diets that they want to follow (keto, carnivore, Mediterranean, whatever type of diet they want to follow), and the AI coach will create a meal plan with those parameters.
They'll also have new things like barcode scanning, a feature where they can go to any menu at any restaurant in the world and take a picture of the menu. The AI coach will tell them the best option on that menu and exactly how to order it from the waiter, with an exact talk track.
For the provider, it's going to give them a full operating system and visibility into how to effectively manage their patients. They can, on the spot in the practice, create a treatment plan, share it with their patient on their phone, click/tap to pay, and let their patient set up a subscription in-app. The app will also manage those subscriptions, charging their cards on a monthly basis and submitting payment directly to the provider's bank account, along with supplement and medication subscriptions that they can set up.Payment processing will be built right in, so they can show the "as low as" price, not the total cost.
Enhancing the business analytics so they will truly have a command center where they can see all of their patients, down to how healthy the patients are. Each patient will have a health score, and it will show how engaged they are in the app. Are they logging their food? Are they talking to the AI coach? Are they opening the app? If they're not doing any of these things, it flags those and will notify the provider and the care team to reach out to them. The provider gets smart notifications if the patient is saying they're not having a good experience or they're saying concerning things to the AI coach. All really focus on providing the best clinical outcomes, as well as keeping the patient in the practice for as long as possible.
