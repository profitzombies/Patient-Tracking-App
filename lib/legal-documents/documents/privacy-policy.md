# Privacy Policy

THINNR is a patient program app offered through the medical practice or med spa that enrolled you (your "office"). This policy explains what the THINNR app and the THINNR operations service collect, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the THINNR mobile app and to app.trythinnr.com.

Your office is responsible for your medical care and keeps your medical record under its own notice of privacy practices. THINNR processes information on your office's behalf to run your program and provides the tools described below.

## What we collect

**Account and contact details.** Your name, date of birth, mobile number, email address, time zone, and the office you are enrolled with. Sign-in happens with a one-time code sent to your phone or email, or with a password you choose.

**Program and health information you enter.** Your weight, body measurements, food and water diary, daily check-ins, medication schedule and doses you record, the diet program you follow with your office, bloodwork results you upload, progress photos you take, and messages you exchange with your care team or the AI coach.

**Information from Apple Health or Health Connect, only if you turn it on.** Weight and step counts that you approve for sharing. THINNR reads these values to save you typing them in; it never writes anything back. See the Health data notice for the exact permissions.

**Photos you choose to share with the coach.** Meal, menu, and nutrition-label photos are analyzed only when the optional "Photo analysis" setting is on. You can turn it off at any time from Account.

**Purchases.** When you buy products from your office through the app, your order, shipping address, and payment status. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored by THINNR.

**Device and usage information.** App version, platform, approximate activity (for example, when the app was last opened), notification tokens, and technical logs that help us keep the service secure and working.

## How we use it

- To run your program: targets, diary, check-ins, reminders, and progress views.
- To let your care team see your progress, answer your messages, and adjust your plan.
- To power the AI coach, which answers food and program questions using your plan and what you log. Coach conversations are processed by an AI provider under a contract that prohibits training on your data.
- To send notifications you have enabled (check-in reminders, care-team replies, order updates).
- To process orders, shipping, and receipts for products your office sells.
- To protect the service: fraud and abuse prevention, security monitoring, and audit trails of who viewed your record.
- To meet legal and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## Who can see it

- **Your office's care team.** Staff at the office that enrolled you can view your program record, including diary, measurements, photos, messages, and orders, so they can care for you. Every staff view of a patient record is logged.
- **THINNR staff.** A small number of THINNR employees can access records to support your office, investigate problems, and maintain the service. Their access is logged and limited to what the task needs.
- **Service providers.** Companies that host data, deliver text messages and email, process payments (Stripe), ship orders, and run the AI coach. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
- **When required by law.** We disclose information when a valid legal process requires it, or to protect the safety of a person.

## How long we keep it

Your office must keep clinical records for the period its state law requires, so program information that forms part of your medical record is retained for that period even after you leave the program. Chat messages, notification history, and technical logs are kept for shorter, fixed periods. When you close your account (see below), everything that is not required to be kept is permanently deleted after a short grace window.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and download your data.** From Account, request a copy of your data. We prepare an archive you can download.
- **Correct it.** Edit your profile, diary, and measurements in the app, or ask your office to correct your record.
- **Turn features off.** Photo analysis, Apple Health or Health Connect sharing, and each kind of notification can be switched off at any time.
- **Close your account.** From Account, or at app.trythinnr.com/delete-account, request deletion. The request stays open for a grace period so you can change your mind; after that, your account is permanently deleted and your office keeps only what it is legally required to keep.
- **Ask us.** Contact details are below. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under state privacy law; we honor these requests regardless of where you live.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Sign-in uses one-time codes, optional passwords with breach checks, and device unlock where you enable it. Staff accounts can be required to use a second factor, and staff sessions expire when idle. Access to patient records is restricted by office and recorded in an audit trail.

## Children

THINNR is intended for adults enrolled by a medical office. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, its version and effective date change with it and the app asks you to review and agree to the new version before you continue. Earlier versions remain available on request.

## Contact

Questions and requests about your information: support@trythinnr.com, or write to THINNR Support at the address shown on app.trythinnr.com/support.
