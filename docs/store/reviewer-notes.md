## Notes to the reviewer

THINNR is a medically supervised weight-loss program used by patients of
subscribing clinics ("offices"). Accounts are invitation-only: a clinic
invites each patient, so the app has no public sign-up. The one app contains
two experiences, a patient app and a clinic staff app. We have prepared one
review account for each inside a dedicated review clinic. Nothing you do in
these accounts reaches a real patient or clinic, and payments are in Stripe
Test mode.

THINNR normally signs users in with a one-time code sent by text or email.
For review, the two accounts below use a fixed code that is **not** sent
anywhere. Type it when the app asks for the code. No phone is needed.

**Patient account**
1. Open the app → **Log In**.
2. Email: `<PATIENT_EMAIL>` · Password: `<PATIENT_PASSWORD>` → **Continue**.
3. Verification code: `<PATIENT_CODE>`.
You land on **Today** with two weeks of sample diary history already logged.

**Clinic staff account**
1. Open the app → **Log In** → **Provider login** (link under the form).

### Permissions and why

- **Camera / Photos** — meal, menu, label, lab-report and guided progress photos. Never used in the background.
- **Motion** (iOS) — device tilt during guided progress photos only.
- **Health** — reads weight and step count only, only after the patient taps *Connect health data*. The app writes nothing to Health.
- **Notifications** — medication reminders, check-in nudges and clinic messages.
- **Face ID / biometrics** — optional app lock, local only.
- **Location** (staff, when Tap to Pay is enabled in a future build) — required by Stripe to accept in-person cards; not requested in this build.
- **No** tracking, advertising SDKs or third-party analytics.

### Health and medical content

THINNR supports a clinician-supervised program. The app does not diagnose,
prescribe or adjust medication; the AI coach is limited to food logging,
recipes and program guidance, and it refers medical questions to the
patient's clinic. See the medical disclaimer in the description.
