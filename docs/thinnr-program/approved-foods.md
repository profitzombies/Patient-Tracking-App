      Thinnr-Approved Protein Selections (Must Be Consumed At Each Meal)

      Please remember that the amount allowed during the metabolic thinning phase is 5-6 oz. If you are
      eating lunchmeat, it needs to be 93/7 or leaner or marked as “Heart Healthy.”

      Protein Hints: when buying lean (93/7 or leaner or marked as “Heart Healthy) lunch meat, go to the
      deli counter and have the clerk package it in 5-6 ounce servings. When buying steak or any other
      protein, ask the butcher to cut it into 5-6 ounce portions and package them separately.

      You can marinate meats. Place meat in marinade for 8-24 hours before cooking and refrigerate.

      Before cooking, rinse the meat under hot water to remove the sugar and fat from the marinade while
      allowing the flavor that has penetrated the meat to remain.

      Feel free to add various seasonings or sauces if they are 0-calorie.

      Please remember that the amount allowed during the Metabolic Thinning phase is 5-6 ounces. All
      protein should be 93% FAT-FREE!

Low-Glycemic & Anti-Inflammatory Foods

| FOOD | RECOMMENDED AMOUNT | KEY BENEFITS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EGGS (PASTURE-RAISED IF POSSIBLE) | 1–2 | High-quality protein, choline; anti-inflammatory if not fried in processed oils |
| SWEET POTATOES | 3–6 oz | Slow-digesting carbs with beta-carotene |
| AVOCADO | ½ medium avocado | Healthy fats + fiber; virtually no blood sugar spike |
| CHERRIES (TART) | 3–6 oz | Potent anti-inflammatory compounds |
| LENTILS (GREEN/BROWN) | 3 oz if eating other complex carbs; 5–6 oz if no other complex carbs | Low-GI, plant-based protein |
| QUINOA | 3 oz if eating other complex carbs; 5–6 oz if no other complex carbs | Complete protein; lower GI than most grains |
| WILD-CAUGHT SALMON OR OTHER OILY FISH) | 6–8 oz | Rich in omega-3 fatty acids |
| ATLANTIC OR SPANISH MACKEREL | 6–8 oz | Omega-3-rich oily fish |
| RAW OR LIGHTLY ROASTED NUTS (ALMONDS, PISTACHIOS, WALNUTS) | 1–2 oz | Healthy fats, fiber, and antioxidants |
| SEEDS (CHIA, FLAX, HEMP) | 1–2 tbsp | Healthy fats, fiber, omega-3s |
