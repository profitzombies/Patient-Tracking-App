                    Thinnr™ - The 2 Pillar Approach to
                    Weight Loss & Weight Maintenance
      The Thinnr Weight Loss Program pillars are the Thinnr Homeopathic Medication and the Thinnr
      Weight Loss & Weight Maintenance Nutrition Strategy.

      The Thinner homeopathic medication is an all-natural, FDA-registered homeopathic OTC
      medicine that supports healthy weight loss, a balanced metabolism, lower stress levels, reduced
      hunger and cravings, detoxification, and improved elimination as part of a comprehensive weight
      management plan. The Thinnr Weight Loss & Weight Maintenance Nutrition Strategy is a science-
      backed nutrition approach emphasizing specific portions and combinations of low glycemic
      index and anti-inflammatory foods. This holistic combination promotes sustainable weight loss,
      reduces inflammation, and supports overall health, making it an ideal solution for long-term weight
      management and well-being.

      The Thinnr program is clinically shown to achieve significant weight loss faster and at a lower cost
      than GLP-1s. As an added benefit, with Thinnr, there are no prescription drugs, no giving yourself
      injections, no strenuous exercise, no shakes, and no expensive prepackaged foods.

      The 4 Phases Of The Thinnr Weight Loss Program

      The Thinnr program is divided into 4 phases: the Metabolic Activation Phase, the Metabolic Thinning
      Phase, the Metabolic Transition Phase, and the Metabolic Normalization Phase. Each phase is
      discussed later in this book.

      Taking the Thinnr Homeopathic Formulation
      The Thinnr formula is administered sublingually (under the tongue) for maximum absorption. Before
      taking it, giving the bottle a quick shake is important.

      You will start taking the formulation three times a day from days 1-39. You will take 1mL three times
      per day. On days 40-42, you won’t take the Thinnr medicine.

      Try to space the doses about 7 hours apart. A suggested schedule is 8 a.m., 3 p.m., and 10 p.m., but if
      those times don’t work for you, choose times that fit your schedule. Just make sure the doses are as
      evenly spaced as possible, around 7 hours apart.

      Avoid eating or drinking for 5 minutes before and after taking the formulation.

      It’s important that you do not skip doses. Missing doses may leave you feeling hungry, may cause
      your blood sugar to fluctuate, and can potentially slow your metabolism. If you miss a dose, please
      take it as soon as possible.

      Exercise During The Thinnr Weight Loss Program
      While you are participating in the Thinnr Weight Loss Program, exercise is not required, however, all
      Zone 1 and Zone 2 exercises are allowed. These include activities such as light to moderated walking
      for 20 minutes and light yoga.

      Because exercise is important for weight maintenance and overall health, it is recommended that you
      either resume your previous exercise routine of start doing Zone 2 and Zone 3 exercise.

      METABOLIC ACTIVATION PHASE (Day 1 and Day 2)
      Drop Dosage: 1mL, 3 times per day

      The goal of the Metabolic Activation Phase is to stimulate and maintain dietary thermogenesis while
      the Thinnr homeopathic medicine takes effect in your body. When you transition into the Metabolic
      Thinning phase, this will help you burn more calories on a daily basis.

      During this phase, you should consume at least 4,000 calories per day. It is recommended that you
      set a target of 70% of your intake be higher fat protein. If you have a history of gallbladder issues
      or have had your gallbladder removed, consult with your healthcare provider before starting the
      Metabolic Activation Phase.

      It is critical that you “graze” throughout the day rather than sticking to just two or three meals.
      Continuous eating is essential, as trying to load with only three meals may result in slower weight
      loss during the next phase.

      You will start drinking 80-100 ounces of water per day. It is important that you spread your water
      consumption across the day.

      In addition to the specific and balanced combination of low-glycemic and anti-inflammatory foods,
      the Thinnr weight loss program utilizes the concepts of intermittent fasting. This means you will eat
      2 times per day, and your goal is to eat both meals within a 6 to 7-hour window of time. For example,
      if you eat your first meal at noon, you will want to ensure you have eaten your second meal by 7:00
      PM. We recommend that you eat lunch and dinner so that a large portion of your intermittent fasting
      is while you sleep. You can still enjoy coffee, water, iced tea, or diet soda, but no added sugar or fats.
      If you need cream for your coffee, opt for a non-fat, calorie-free version and a sweetener with Stevia
      instead of sugar. If a food is not on the “approved list”, it is not allowed UNLESS is it specifically
      called for in a Thinnr-approved recipe.

      It is important to vary and rotate your protein, vegetable, and fruit selections for each meal. Basically,
      avoid eating the same food multiple meals in a row, meaning don’t eat chicken as your protein
      selection for more than 2 consecutive meals.

      To make this easy to understand, we will assume that your first meal will be lunch and your second
      meal will be dinner. We will also assume that Your first meal will be eaten at noon and your 2nd meal
      will be consumed at 6:00 PM.

                       Vegetarian And Vegan Guidelines
      Thanks to the wide variety of high-quality vegetarian and vegan products now available, the Thinnr
      Weight Loss Program is no longer considered difficult for those following plant-based diets.

      Below are the specific guidelines for vegetarians and vegans participating in the Thinnr Weight Loss
      Program. Due to the unique nature of plant-based diets, certain recommendations will differ from the
      standard Thinnr plan.

      When incorporating a meat substitute, the serving size should be limited to 5-6 ounces. Since many
      plant-based meat alternatives contain more fat than what is typically allowed during the Thinnr
      Metabolic Thinning Phase, it is important to limit these to one meal per day. The other meal should be
      lower in fat to balance the intake.

      There is no need to measure your vegetable portions for meals that do not include meat substitutes.
      Eat enough low-starch vegetables to feel satisfied—no weighing necessary. Free vegetables, as
      listed in the program, are great options for helping you feel full.

      • Meal preparation is essential! Prepare soups and multi-serving meals beforehand, and freeze them
        in portion-sized containers for convenience.
      • When using vegetarian or vegan meat alternatives, stick to the 5-6 ounce serving size 		
        recommended for the Thinnr Metabolic Thinning Phase.
      • Because meat alternatives are generally higher in fat, limit them to one meal a day, making sure
        the other meal is low in fat.
      • Avoid starchy vegetables like corn, peas, and potatoes.

      Approved vegetarian and vegan products that can replace the protein options listed in
      the Thinnr recipes include:

      •   Vegetarian chicken alternatives
      •   Vegetarian beef alternatives
      •   Vegetarian pork alternatives
      •   Vegetarian sausage and hotdog alternatives
      •   Tofu and tempeh
      •   Edamame (limit to 1-2 times per week)
      •   Vegetarian/vegan broths
      •   Skim milk
      •   Cottage cheese
      •   Unsweetened yogurt
      •   If you regularly eat eggs, egg whites or egg substitutes are an excellent source of protein.
